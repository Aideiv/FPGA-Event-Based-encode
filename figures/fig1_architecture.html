Fig. 1 System Architecture: Dual-Pipeline Collision Avoidance
Complete drone collision avoidance architecture. Event camera data streams into FPGA fabric for real-time normal flow estimation (VecKM, d=128). The ARM CPU runs two parallel pipelines: (1) VecKM flow-based object detection and TTC estimation at ~100Hz, and (2) a direct CNN action predictor at ~1kHz. Outputs are fused into graded evasion velocity commands for the quadrotor motors.
